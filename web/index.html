!

Experimental model — use for evaluation only

Held-out accuracy was 51.25%; recall was 12.0% for normal, 49.9% for overhang and 56.0% for underhang. The model can miss faults and raise false alarms. Do not use it as a safety interlock or replacement for inspection.
